Who we are

A Simpler Way to Arrange Transportation in Italy

Italy Taxi Service is focused on one thing: helping travellers get from one place to another in Italy without the guesswork. You tell us where you are starting and where you need to go, and we arrange private transportation around that.

That covers airport transfers, private transfers, city transfers, long-distance journeys, group transportation and chauffeur services. We describe what we offer plainly and we confirm the details of each journey with you before you travel.

Our approach

We Start With the Journey, Not the Vehicle

A transport arrangement only makes sense once we understand the trip. So we begin with the questions below, and the right arrangement follows from the answers.

  1. Where is the traveller starting?
  2. Where are they going?
  3. When do they need to travel?
  4. How many people are travelling?
  5. How much luggage do they have?
  6. Is there anything important about the journey?

Why it matters

Italy Is Easier to Enjoy When the Journey Is Clear

Travelling in Italy is part of the pleasure, and also part of the planning. Streets you do not know, bags you have to carry, a change of hotel, an airport arrival, a railway connection, a language you do not speak, a group that needs to stay together, a move from one city to the next: none of these is a problem on its own, but they add up over a trip.

Public transport works well for many of those moments. For travellers who prefer a private arrangement, Italy Taxi Service is one option: a ride planned around your details, so there is one less thing to work out.

Questions

About Italy Taxi Service

What does Italy Taxi Service provide?

Private transportation in Italy, arranged around the traveller's journey: airport transfers, private transfers, city transfers, long-distance journeys, group transfers and chauffeur service.

Which types of transportation can I book?

Airport transfers, private transfers, city transfers, long-distance transfers, group and minivan transfers, and chauffeur service. Availability for a particular route is confirmed when you send your request.

Can I book airport transfers?

Yes. You can request a ride between an airport and your hotel, address or destination. Include your flight details so the pickup can be planned. See airport transfers.

Can I travel between Italian cities?

Yes, you can request a long-distance transfer between cities. Send your starting point, destination and date, and we will confirm whether we can take the journey.

Can families and groups book transportation?

Yes. Tell us how many people are travelling and what luggage you have, and we will suggest a suitable arrangement. See group transfers.

How do I request a transfer?

Share your pickup, destination, date, time, passengers and luggage through the booking form. We review your request and contact you with the next steps.

Where can I find the booking form?

The booking form is on our booking page.
