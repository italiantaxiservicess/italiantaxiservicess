Destinations

Five Places Travellers Ask About

These are the destinations we list. Rome has a dedicated page; the others will follow, and until then you can still send a route for any of them.

  1. 01

    Rome

    Rome is a city of layers: ancient sites, busy modern streets and a historic centre where vehicle access can differ from one street to the next. That makes a clear pickup point and exact destination more useful here than almost anywhere. Our Rome page covers airports, hotels, stations and sightseeing in detail.

    Rome taxi & transfers
  2. 02

    Milan

    Milan works to a business rhythm, with meetings, trade events and tight connections to flights and trains. Travellers here tend to care about arriving at a set time, so the details of the appointment matter as much as the address.

    Dedicated page coming soon · See it on the homepage

  3. 03

    Florence

    Florence is compact and best seen slowly, but its historic core is narrow and often restricted to traffic. Visitors frequently stay in the centre and want to reach the wider Tuscan countryside or the next city, which is where a pre-arranged ride can help.

    Dedicated page coming soon · See it on the homepage

  4. 04

    Venice

    Venice is unlike any other Italian destination: the historic centre has canals instead of roads, so the journey by road ends at its edge and the last stretch is made another way. That is worth thinking through before you book, so tell us exactly where you are staying.

    Dedicated page coming soon · See it on the homepage

  5. 05

    Naples

    Naples sits on its bay beneath Vesuvius and is a starting point for the Amalfi Coast, Pompeii and the islands. Many travellers pass through on their way somewhere else, so onward journeys are as common here as arrivals.

    Dedicated page coming soon · See it on the homepage

Travel planning

How the Pieces of a Trip Fit Together

Most trips in Italy are a chain of short and long moves. You arrive, settle in, explore, then travel on. Each link has its own kind of transfer:

  1. AirportArrival, with an airport transfer to where you are staying.
  2. HotelA base in the city, and a city transfer when you need to cross town.
  3. CityDays spent on foot and by short rides, sometimes with a chauffeur for a planned day.
  4. Next destinationA long-distance transfer to the next city.

Questions

About Destinations and Coverage

Which destinations are listed on the website?

We show Rome, Milan, Florence, Venice and Naples. Rome has its own page, and the others are listed as destinations while their pages are prepared. Listing a city is not a promise that every service is available there.

Do you cover every city in Italy?

We do not claim that. We are confirming where we operate, so send us your route and we will tell you whether we can arrange it.

Can I request an airport transfer to one of these cities?

You can send a request. Which airports we can serve is confirmed when you submit it, and the airport transfers page explains what to include.

Can I travel between two of the cities listed?

You can ask. Choose Long-Distance Transfer on the booking page and enter both ends of the journey. See long-distance transfers. Routes on our pages are examples, not confirmed routes.

How do I enquire about a route?

Use the booking page with your pickup, destination, date, time, passengers and luggage, or ask a question first through the contact page. We review it and contact you with the next steps.

Will I see a price or journey time for a route?

No. We do not publish route prices or journey durations here, and the booking form does not show an automatic fare. Trip-specific details are confirmed after you send your request.
