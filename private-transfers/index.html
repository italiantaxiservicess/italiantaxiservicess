The idea

Travel Directly, Without the Extra Steps

With a private transfer, you tell us where you are starting and where you are going. The journey is arranged around those two points and your travel details, and you ride straight through, without working out connections or carrying bags from one vehicle to the next.

It suits the trips that do not fit neatly on a timetable: a hotel-to-hotel move between cities, an airport-to-hotel ride after a late landing, a city-to-city journey, a private sightseeing day, a family with a lot of luggage, or a business trip with a meeting at the other end.

Nothing here changes what you need to know in advance. Once you send the details, we confirm the journey with you.

Questions

Private Transfer Questions

Short, direct answers first.

What is a private transfer?

A pre-arranged ride for your own party, from a pickup point you choose directly to your destination. You do not share the vehicle with other travellers or change between transport options.

Can I book a private transfer between Italian cities?

Yes, you can request a city-to-city journey. Send your starting point, destination and date, and we will confirm whether we can take the route and the details. See long-distance transfers.

Can I travel from an airport directly to another destination?

Yes. Request a ride from the airport to a hotel, an address or a destination beyond the arrival city, and include your flight details so the pickup can be planned around your arrival. More on airport transfers.

Can families book private transfers?

Yes. Tell us how many adults and children are travelling, how many bags you have and whether you need a child seat, so we can suggest a suitable vehicle.

Can I book a private transfer for a business trip?

Yes. Send the pickup address, the destination and the time you need to arrive, and we will work out the journey around it.

What information do I need to provide when booking?

Your pickup location, destination, date, preferred time, number of passengers, luggage information, flight details when relevant, and contact information.

How is the price of a private transfer determined?

It depends on factors such as the route, the type of vehicle or service and your travel details. Send your journey details and we will confirm the price for your specific transfer.
