After you land

From Landing to Your Destination

The flight is the part everyone plans. The hour after it is where trips tend to wobble: you are tired, the terminal is unfamiliar, the bags are heavy and the hotel is across a city you have never driven in.

A pre-arranged transfer takes most of those decisions off the table before you board. Your destination and pickup details are agreed in advance, so once you land you are following a plan instead of building one. It is not magic, and it is not the only way to get from an airport to a hotel, but it is a straightforward one.

Questions

Airport Transfer Questions

Direct answers to what travellers ask before they book.

How do I book an airport transfer in Italy?

Send your airport, destination, date, time, number of passengers and contact details using the request form. We will confirm the transfer with you and send the pickup information.

What information should I provide when booking?

The airport, whether you are arriving or departing, your flight number if you have one, the pickup date and time, your destination, how many passengers are travelling, how much luggage you have and a way to contact you.

Can I book an airport transfer to a hotel?

Yes. Give us the hotel name and full address. If the hotel is in a pedestrian area or an old town centre, mention it so the drop-off point can be agreed in advance.

Can families book a private airport transfer?

Yes. Tell us how many adults and children are travelling, how many bags you have and whether you need a child seat, so we can arrange a suitable vehicle.

Can I arrange an airport transfer for an early flight?

Yes, you can request an early-morning pickup. Send your flight time and terminal if you know it, and book as far ahead as you can so the pickup time can be worked out properly.

Can I travel from an airport to another Italian city?

Yes. You can request a private transfer from the airport to a city or destination beyond the arrival city. Send your route and date and we will confirm whether we can take the journey.

How much does an airport transfer cost?

The cost depends on the route, the type of vehicle or service and your travel details. Send your journey details and we will confirm the price for your specific transfer.
