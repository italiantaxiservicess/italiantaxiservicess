01

Booking

How requesting a transfer works.

How do I request a transfer?

Use the booking page. Enter your service type, pickup, destination, date, time, number of passengers and your contact details, then send the request. We review it and contact you with the next steps.

What information do I need to provide?

Your pickup location, destination, travel date, preferred pickup time, number of passengers, luggage information and a phone or WhatsApp number. Add any special instructions in the notes. The checklist further down this page covers each item.

Can I ask a question before booking?

Yes. Use the contact page for general questions, route questions or help choosing a service. You do not have to book to ask.

Can I request a transfer for a future date?

Yes. Choose your travel date on the booking page. Dates in the past cannot be selected. Book as early as you can for airport pickups, early departures and busy periods.

Can I change my trip details after submitting a request?

If something changes, contact us as soon as you can through the contact page and tell us what has changed. We will confirm what can be updated.

Where should I go if I need help with a booking?

The contact page is the place for booking help. It also explains which details are most useful to include.

02

Airport Transfers

Arrivals and departures.

Can I request an airport transfer in Italy?

Yes. Choose Airport Transfer on the booking page and enter the airport as your pickup or destination. See airport transfers for more. We confirm which airports we can serve when you send your request.

What information should I provide for an airport pickup?

The airport, your flight number if you have one, the date and arrival time, your destination, the number of passengers and your luggage. Pickup instructions are agreed with you when the booking is confirmed.

Can I request an airport-to-hotel transfer?

Yes. Give us the hotel name and full address as your destination. If the hotel is in a pedestrian or restricted area, mention it so a sensible drop-off point can be agreed.

Can I request a hotel-to-airport transfer?

Yes. Enter your hotel as the pickup and the airport as the destination, with your flight time and terminal if you know it, so the pickup can be planned.

What should I do if I am unsure about my pickup details?

Send what you have and say what is still open. You can also ask through the contact page. We will tell you what is missing.

03

Private & City Transfers

Direct rides and rides around town.

What is a private transfer?

A pre-arranged ride for your own party, from a pickup point you choose directly to your destination, without sharing the vehicle or changing between transport options. Read more on private transfers.

What is a city transfer?

A ride within or around a city, for example between a hotel, station, attraction or meeting. See city transfers.

Can I travel between hotels and stations?

Yes. Enter the hotel and the station as pickup and destination, and add your train time so the pickup can be planned with some margin.

Can I request transportation to attractions or other destinations?

Yes. Tell us where you are starting and where you want to go. Busy historic areas may have specific access or pickup arrangements, so the meeting point may be nearby. A transfer does not include a guide or tour.

Can I request a private transfer between different Italian cities?

Yes. Send your starting point, destination and date, and we will confirm whether we can take the journey. See long-distance transfers.

04

Long-Distance Transfers

Between cities and regions.

Can I travel between cities by private transfer?

You can request one. Choose Long-Distance Transfer on the booking page and enter both ends of the journey.

Are long-distance transfers available?

They are one of the services we offer, and availability for a specific route is confirmed when you send it. Example routes on our pages show the kind of journey you can ask about, not a list of confirmed routes.

Can I request hotel-to-hotel transportation?

Yes. Give both hotel names and addresses, plus your check-out and expected check-in times, and the journey can be planned around them.

Can I ask about a specific route before booking?

Yes. Ask through the contact page. We do not publish prices or journey times for routes on this site.

05

Groups & Minivans

Travelling together.

Can groups request a private transfer?

Yes. Choose Group & Minivan Transfer and enter the number of passengers. See group transfers.

Can I include luggage information in my request?

Yes, and please do. The booking form has a luggage field, and the notes box is the place for large or unusual items.

How should a group provide passenger details?

Give the total number of passengers, say how many are children if relevant, and name one contact person who will be reachable on the day.

Can I ask about vehicle suitability for my group?

Yes. Tell us your group size and luggage and we will suggest what can be arranged. We do not list vehicle models or capacities on this site.

06

Chauffeur Service

A driver for the plan.

What is a chauffeur service?

A driver arranged around a planned journey, rather than a single point-to-point ride. See chauffeur service.

When might a chauffeur service be useful?

On days with several legs: business visits, events, sightseeing days or occasions with a fixed arrival time.

Can I ask about a chauffeur requirement before booking?

Yes. Share your date, hours and plan through the contact page and we will tell you what can be arranged.

07

Pricing & Quotes

How to get trip-specific pricing.

Will I see a price when I submit a request?

No. The booking form does not display an automatic estimated fare. Journey details are submitted first so the request can be reviewed.

How do I find out the price of my transfer?

Submit your trip details on the booking page, or ask a route question on the contact page. Pricing depends on factors such as the route, the type of vehicle or service and your travel details.

How quickly will I get a reply?

We do not state a response time. We review each request and contact you with the next steps.

Where do you operate in Italy?

We are confirming our service areas, so we do not list a fixed coverage here. Send your route and we will confirm whether we can arrange it. We have a dedicated page for Rome.
