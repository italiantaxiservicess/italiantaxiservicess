Naples Taxi & Private Transfers

Naples is loud, layered and brilliant, and arriving in it can be a lot: a busy airport, a station crowd, a hotel down a lane that cars barely enter. A pre-arranged private ride can take some of that guesswork out of the first and last hour. Four kinds of journey

Know Which Ride You Are Asking For

In Naples, the shape of the journey changes what we need from you. Each of these has a different anchor: a flight, a train, an address, or a longer road.

Anchored by a flight

Airport transfer

Naples International Airport (NAP) to your hotel or onward, or back for a departure. Your flight number and time are what the plan is built around. See airport transfers.

Anchored by a train

Station pickup

Napoli Centrale is where many visitors arrive, often straight into a crowd. Your train time and the exact exit or meeting spot matter most.

Anchored by an address

Hotel journey

Between your accommodation and anywhere else in the city. In the historic centre the exact street decides where a vehicle can stop. See city transfers.

A fourth kind, a direct longer ride, is covered under private transfers and long-distance transfers.

Arriving at NAP

Why the Meeting Point Matters More Than You Think

After a flight, the last thing you want is a search. Airports have several exits and areas, and arrivals can be crowded, so the exact terminal or agreed meeting point is the detail that makes a pickup work.

We do not state a default meeting point here, and we do not promise that a driver will wait for any particular time. Those details are agreed with you for your journey, once we know your flight and where you are going.

  1. Send your flight detailsNumber, date and arrival time, plus the airport.
  2. Say where you are headingHotel name and full address, or the next destination.
  3. Agree the meeting pointWe confirm it with you before you travel.
  4. Keep the details to handYour contact number and the agreed spot, on the day.

The historic centre

Narrow Streets and Where a Car Can Stop

The old centre of Naples is a tight web of lanes, many of them narrow and some with restricted vehicle access. The rules differ from street to street, and we make no claims about them here.

What helps is precision. Send your accommodation's exact name and address, and say how you are carrying luggage. We then agree a realistic pickup or drop-off point rather than promising the doorstep, and you know in advance whether the last stretch is a short walk.

Questions

Naples Transfer Questions

How do I request a transfer from Naples International Airport (NAP)?

Send a request on the booking page with NAP as your pickup, plus your flight number and arrival time, your destination in or around Naples, passengers and luggage. Whether we can serve NAP is confirmed when you submit. We do not describe meeting-point instructions or waiting time here; those are agreed with you. See airport transfers.

Why does the meeting point at the airport matter?

Airports have several exits and areas, and arrivals can be busy. Agreeing exactly where you will meet avoids a search after a long flight. We confirm the meeting point with you for your journey; we do not state a default one on this page.

Can I be picked up at Napoli Centrale railway station?

You can enquire. Name the station, the exit or side where you will wait, and your train time. We confirm with you whether a particular spot can be used and do not promise one in advance.

Are there streets in the historic centre a car cannot enter?

Yes, parts of Naples' historic centre have narrow streets and areas with restricted vehicle access, and the rules differ from street to street. Give your accommodation's exact name and address, and we agree a realistic pickup or drop-off point rather than promising the doorstep.

Can I travel with large luggage in Naples?

Often yes, but plan for the last stretch. Narrow lanes, steps and cobbles can mean a short walk from where a vehicle stops. Tell us the number of bags and mention anything large, with how many passengers. We state no luggage capacities. See group transfers for larger parties.

Can I enquire about Pompeii, Sorrento or the Amalfi Coast?

Yes, you can ask. Name the destination, the date and the group size, and we confirm whether we can arrange it. These places are examples of enquiries, not confirmed routes, and we publish no journey times or prices. See long-distance transfers.

What should I include when asking for a quote?

Exact pickup and drop-off addresses, travel date, pickup time, number of passengers and luggage. For airports and stations add your flight or train number. The website shows no automatic estimated fare; pricing is confirmed for your specific journey.

What is the difference between an airport transfer, a station pickup and a private journey?

An airport transfer starts or ends at an airport and works around a flight. A station pickup works around a train and a meeting spot. A private transfer is a direct ride between two places you choose, and a city transfer is a shorter ride within the city.
