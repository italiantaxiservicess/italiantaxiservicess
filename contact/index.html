Questions

Before You Get in Touch

What information should I include when contacting you?

Say what you would like to know and, if it is about a journey, where you are starting, where you are going and roughly when. For a full transfer request, the booking page asks for everything we need.

Should I use the contact form or the booking form?

Use the contact form for questions. Use the booking page when you have your trip details ready and want to request a transfer.

Can I ask about a specific route before booking?

Yes. Send your route as a question and we will tell you whether we can arrange it. Examples on our pages show the kind of journey you can ask about, not confirmed routes.

Can I contact you about airport transfers?

Yes. Include the airport and, if you have it, your flight details. You can also read more on the airport transfers page.

Can I ask about group travel requirements?

Yes. Tell us how many people are travelling and what luggage you have, and we will tell you what can be arranged. See also the group transfers page.

Planning a Journey in Italy?

Ask us a question, or send your trip details through the booking page. Whichever suits you better.
