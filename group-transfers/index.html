The idea

When Everyone Is Travelling Together

A group is rarely just a headcount. It is children who need to stay close, friends arriving on the same flight, relatives with suitcases of every size, or colleagues heading to the same venue. Splitting them across several cars means several pickups to explain and several arrivals to wait for.

Arranging one transfer for the whole group puts everyone on a single booking and a single pickup plan. It suits families, friends, airport arrivals, moves between hotels, journeys between cities and organised travel to an event. It is not automatically cheaper than separate cars, and we do not say it is; it is simply easier to coordinate.

Scenarios

One Group. One Journey. Fewer Things to Coordinate.

Family Travel

Keeping the family in one vehicle makes pickup, luggage and arrival easier to plan: one address, one time, and nobody left to track down at the other end. Mention children's ages and any car seat needs when you book.

Friends & Small Groups

For travellers who would rather arrive together than arrange separate rides and compare notes on the way. One booking, one pickup point, one set of details to share in the group chat.

Airport Group Transfers

For groups arriving or departing together. Share the flight details and luggage so the pickup can be planned around everyone. Read about airport transfers.

City & Intercity Travel

Moving a group around town is a different task from taking it across regions. See city transfers for shorter hops and long-distance transfers for journeys between destinations.

The luggage question

More People Usually Means More Luggage

Luggage is often what decides which vehicle suits a group, not the number of seats. Tell us what you are bringing:

  • Suitcases
  • Carry-on bags
  • Children's luggage
  • Larger bags
  • Special or unusual items

Please give accurate luggage details when you book. We do not state a luggage capacity here, and we cannot promise that a particular vehicle will take a specific number of bags until we know what you have.

Worth knowing

A Few Details Can Make Group Travel Easier

Choose a clear pickup point
Large hotels, stations and busy streets can have complicated pickup areas, so name an entrance or landmark.
Keep everyone informed
Make sure the whole group knows the pickup time and location.
Mention luggage early
Extra or unusual luggage can affect transportation planning.
Keep contact information available
Make sure the booking contact can be reached when needed.
Allow time for everyone
Groups may take longer to gather, load luggage and leave a location.

Questions

Group Transfer Questions

Direct answers to what groups ask first.

What is a group transfer?

A pre-arranged private ride for a family, friends or a larger party, so that everyone travels together from a shared pickup point to a shared destination under one booking.

Can families book a private group transfer in Italy?

Yes. Tell us how many adults and children are travelling, how much luggage you have and whether you need a child seat, and we will suggest a suitable arrangement.

Can I book transportation for friends travelling together?

Yes. Send one pickup, one destination and the number of people, and the group travels on a single booking instead of several separate rides.

How many passengers can travel in one vehicle?

That depends on the vehicle, and we do not publish capacities on this page. Tell us the size of your group and we will confirm what can be arranged for you.

Can we travel with several suitcases?

Often, yes, but it depends on the vehicle and what you are bringing. List your bags, including anything large or unusual, so we can consider the right arrangement before you travel.

Can groups book airport transfers?

Yes. Include the airport, flight details, number of passengers and luggage so the pickup can be planned around the whole group. More on airport transfers is on our airport transfers page.

Can groups travel between Italian cities?

Yes, you can request a journey between cities for a group. Send your starting point, destination and date, and we will confirm whether we can take the route.

What information should I provide when booking a group transfer?

Pickup location, destination, date, preferred pickup time, number of passengers, luggage details, any children's travel requirements, and special instructions.

Can I request special arrangements for children or luggage?

You can ask. Mention child seats or unusual luggage when you book, and we will tell you what can be arranged for your journey.
