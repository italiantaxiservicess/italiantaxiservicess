Why go private

Some Italian Journeys Are Better Planned From Start to Finish

Italy is well connected, and trains suit many city-to-city trips. But some journeys are awkward to piece together: the hotel is not near a station, the next one is in a small town, you land at an airport and need to reach another region, or you are travelling with children and a lot of luggage.

A private long-distance transfer lets you go door to door in one vehicle. You might use it to move between cities, to change hotels, to continue after landing, or for business travel between meetings in different places. It will not always be the quickest or cheapest choice, and we do not claim it is. It is simply a different way to cover the distance.

Scenarios

When a Long-Distance Transfer Makes Sense

01

City to City

For travellers moving from one Italian city to another. You leave from the door of one hotel or address and arrive at the next, with no station transfers at either end.

02

Airport to Another Destination

For journeys that continue beyond the airport. Land, collect your bags and carry on to a town or region further away, in a single ride. See airport transfers.

03

Hotel to Hotel

Useful when changing accommodation between destinations. Check-out time and check-in time become the two points to plan around.

04

Family Travel

For groups who prefer to remain together during a longer journey, with children and luggage in one place instead of across several connections.

05

Business Travel

For travellers moving between meetings, offices or events in different cities, where a fixed arrival time matters more than the scenery.

06

Leisure Itineraries

For travellers building a multi-destination Italian trip, where each leg is a separate private journey from one stay to the next.

Luggage

Bring What You Need for the Journey

On a longer trip you tend to pack for the whole trip, not a weekend. Suitcases, carry-on bags, a pile of family luggage, a pushchair or something bulky like sports gear: all of it affects which vehicle suits the journey.

Tell us about significant luggage when you book so we can consider the appropriate vehicle. We do not promise a particular capacity, and not every vehicle can take every type of luggage, so the more you tell us up front, the better.

Between places

The Road Is Part of the Journey

Going from one part of Italy to another means crossing hills, coastline and countryside. Staying in one vehicle for the whole trip means you are not stopping to change transport halfway, and you arrive where you are staying rather than where the line ends.

Before You Set Off

Check the pickup point
Make sure the driver can identify the correct pickup location. A named entrance or landmark helps.
Allow realistic travel time
Road conditions and traffic can affect longer journeys, so avoid planning the day too tightly.
Share luggage information
Especially if you are travelling with several suitcases or unusual items.
Keep your travel details available
Have your booking information and destination details to hand on the day.
Plan around connections
If the journey connects with a flight, train or event, leave appropriate time between the arrangements.

Questions

Long-Distance Transfer Questions

Direct answers to what travellers ask first.

What is a long-distance transfer?

A pre-arranged private journey over a longer distance, such as between two Italian cities or from an airport to a destination in another town, made in one vehicle from your pickup point to your destination.

Can I travel privately between Italian cities?

Yes, you can request a private journey from one city to another. Send your starting point, destination and date, and we will confirm whether we can take the route.

Can I book a transfer from one hotel to another city?

Yes. Give us both hotel names and addresses, plus your check-out and expected check-in times, and the journey can be planned around them.

Can I travel with several suitcases?

Often, yes, but it depends on the vehicle. Tell us how many bags you have and mention anything large or unusual when you book, so the right vehicle can be considered.

Can families book long-distance transfers?

Yes. Let us know how many adults and children are travelling and whether you need a child seat, so the arrangement suits the whole group.

Can I request a specific pickup time?

You can say when you would like to be collected. The time is agreed with you as part of confirming the booking.

What information is needed to arrange a long-distance journey?

The exact pickup location, destination, travel date, preferred pickup time, number of passengers, luggage details, any child or family requirements, and important travel instructions.

Can I book a journey that connects with a flight or train?

Yes. Include your flight or train details and leave appropriate time between the arrangements, since road conditions can affect longer journeys.
