Questions

Before You Book

Short answers to what travellers usually ask first.

How can I book a taxi?

Enter your pickup location, drop-off location, date, time and number of passengers in the booking form, then send your request. We will confirm your booking with you.

Can I book an airport transfer?

Yes. You can request a ride between an airport and your hotel, address or destination. Add your flight number so the pickup can be planned around your arrival.

Do you provide private transfers between cities?

Yes. Private transfers between cities are door-to-door. Enter where you are starting and where you are going, and we will confirm the details with you.

Can I book transportation for a family or group?

Yes. Let us know how many people are travelling and how much luggage you have, so we can suggest the right vehicle, such as a van or minivan for larger parties.

How far in advance should I book?

As early as you can, especially for airport pickups, early departures and busy travel periods. If your plans change at short notice, you can still send a request and we will do our best to help.
