Booking Questions

What information do I need to provide when booking?

Your pickup location, destination, travel date, preferred pickup time, number of passengers, luggage information, your name and a phone or WhatsApp number, plus any special instructions.

Can I request an airport transfer?

Yes. Choose Airport Transfer, enter the airport as your pickup or destination, and add your flight number in the additional information.

Can I book transportation between Italian cities?

Yes. Choose Long-Distance Transfer and enter your starting point and destination. We will confirm whether we can take the journey.

Can I book for a group?

Yes. Choose Group & Minivan Transfer, enter the number of passengers and describe your luggage so we can suggest a suitable arrangement.

Can I include luggage information?

Yes. Use the luggage field and add details about large or unusual items in the additional information.

Can I request a specific pickup time?

Yes. Enter your preferred pickup time. The time is subject to confirmation.

Will I see the price before submitting my request?

The booking form does not display an automatic estimated fare. Journey details are submitted first so the transportation request can be reviewed.
