Booking & Requests

How a request works, from first details to next steps.

How do I request a transfer?

Use the booking page: choose a service type, enter your pickup, destination, date, time and passengers, add your contact details and send the request. We review it and contact you with the next steps. A request is not an automatic confirmation.

What information should I provide when booking?

Your pickup location, destination, travel date, preferred pickup time, number of passengers, luggage information and a phone or WhatsApp number. Notes about access, stops or flight and train details help too. The checklist further down this page lists each item.

Can I request a transfer for a future date?

Yes. Pick your travel date on the booking page; past dates cannot be selected. For airport pickups, early departures and busy travel periods, send your request as early as you can.

Can I ask questions before submitting a booking request?

Yes. The contact page is for general questions, route questions and help choosing a service. You do not have to book to ask.

What should I do if my travel details change?

Tell us as soon as you can through the contact page, saying what has changed, such as the date, time, passengers or destination. We will confirm what can be updated.

Where can I get help with a booking?

The contact page is the place for booking help. If you are not sure which service to pick, the services overview explains each one.

02

Airport Transfers

Arrivals, departures and pickup details.

Can I request a transfer to or from an airport in Italy?

Yes. Choose Airport Transfer on the booking page and enter the airport as your pickup or destination. Which airports we can serve is confirmed when you send your request. More detail is on the airport transfers page.

What details should I provide for an airport pickup?

The airport, your flight number if you have one, the date and arrival time, your destination, the number of passengers and your luggage. Pickup instructions are agreed with you when the booking is confirmed.

Can I request transportation between an airport and my hotel?

Yes, in either direction. Give the hotel name and full address. If the hotel is in a pedestrian or restricted area, say so, so a sensible drop-off or pickup point can be agreed.

What if I am unsure about my pickup location?

Send what you know and say what is still open. You can also ask through the contact page. We will tell you which details we still need.

03

Private & City Transfers

Direct rides, and rides around town.

What is a private transfer?

A pre-arranged ride for your own party, from a pickup point you choose directly to your destination, without sharing the vehicle or changing between transport options. See private transfers.

What is the difference between a city transfer and a long-distance transfer?

A city transfer is a shorter ride within or around one city, such as hotel to station. A long-distance transfer is a longer road journey between cities or regions. See city transfers and long-distance transfers.

Can I request transport between a hotel, train station or attraction?

Yes. Enter the two places as pickup and destination and, for trains, add your departure time so the pickup can be planned with some margin. Busy historic areas may have specific access arrangements, so the meeting point may be nearby. A transfer does not include a guide or tour.

Can I enquire about a specific destination in Italy?

Yes. Send the destination through the contact page or in a booking request and we will confirm whether we can arrange it. We have a dedicated page for Rome.

04

Long-Distance Journeys

Between cities and regions.

Can I request a private transfer between Italian cities?

You can request one. Choose Long-Distance Transfer on the booking page and enter both ends of the journey. Availability for a specific route is confirmed when you send it, and routes shown as examples on our pages are not a list of confirmed routes.

Can I enquire about hotel-to-hotel transportation?

Yes. Give both hotel names and addresses, plus your check-out time and expected check-in time, so the journey can be planned around them.

What information is useful when planning a longer journey?

The exact pickup, the destination, the date and preferred time, passengers, luggage (including anything large), any child seat need, and any connection such as a flight or train. Read more on the long-distance transfers page.

How can I ask about a particular route?

Send it as a question through the contact page, naming the start, destination and date. We do not publish journey times or prices for routes on this site.

05

Groups & Chauffeur Services

Travelling together, and drivers for a planned day.

Can groups enquire about private transportation?

Yes. Choose Group & Minivan Transfer on the booking page or ask through the contact page. See group transfers.

Should I include passenger and luggage details?

Yes, please. Give the total number of passengers, how many are children if relevant, and what luggage you have. Luggage often decides which vehicle suits a group.

How can I check whether a vehicle suits my group?

Tell us your group size and luggage and we will tell you what can be arranged. We do not list vehicle models or capacities on this site, so the suggestion is based on what you send.

What should I ask when enquiring about chauffeur services?

Share your date, the hours you need, the places you plan to visit and the number of people. A chauffeur is a driver arranged around a plan rather than a single ride. See chauffeur service for examples.

06

Pricing & Payment

What we can and cannot say about cost.

How can I request pricing for my journey?

Send your trip details on the booking page, or ask a route question on the contact page. Pricing depends on factors such as the route, the type of vehicle or service and your travel details, and is confirmed for your specific journey.

Does the website calculate an automatic estimated fare?

No. The booking form does not display an automatic estimated fare. Journey details are submitted first so the request can be reviewed.

Which details are needed to discuss a route-specific price?

The pickup, destination, date and time, number of passengers and luggage. Any extra stops or special requirements should be included as well. We do not publish payment methods or pricing policies on this site, so please ask if you need to know.

Service Areas

Where this applies.

How can I check whether my destination is covered?

Send your route and we will confirm. We are confirming our service areas, so we do not list a fixed coverage and do not claim every Italian city is covered.

Can I enquire about a route that is not listed on the website?

Yes. The routes on our pages are examples of the kind of journey you can ask about. Send yours through the contact page and we will tell you whether we can arrange it.

Where can I explore the services currently listed?

Start with the services overview, then see airport transfers, private transfers, city transfers, long-distance transfers, group transfers and chauffeur service. The Rome page is our first city page.
