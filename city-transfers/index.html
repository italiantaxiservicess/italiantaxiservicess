Short trips, big difference

When Getting Across the City Should Be Simple

Most trips in an Italian city are short, and most of them have a deadline. You need to reach your hotel with your bags, catch a train from the station on time, get to a meeting in an unfamiliar district, see an attraction before it gets crowded, move from one accommodation to another, or keep a family together while crossing town with luggage.

A private ride does not make the city smaller, but it does mean you are not working out the route, the ticket and the walk at the end. You give us the two addresses; we arrange the rest. See all services

Worth knowing

A Few Things Worth Checking Before Your Ride

Pickup location
Make sure the pickup point is clear, especially around busy streets, stations and large hotels. A named entrance or a landmark helps.
Luggage
Let us know if you are travelling with unusually large luggage or equipment, so the vehicle suits it.
Time
Allow enough time for traffic and for your appointment or connection. Build in a margin if you have a train to catch.
Contact details
Keep the booking contact information handy on the day, in case you need to reach us or the driver needs to reach you.

Questions

Getting Around the City: Questions

Direct answers to what travellers ask first.

What is a city transfer?

A pre-arranged private ride within or around an Italian city, from a pickup point you give us to a destination you choose, such as a hotel, station, office or attraction.

Can I book a private ride around an Italian city?

Yes. Send your pickup, destination, date and time, and we will confirm the details with you. Check which cities we currently serve before you book, as coverage is being confirmed.

Can I travel from a hotel to a railway station?

Yes. Give us the hotel address and the station, plus your train departure time, so the pickup can be planned with enough margin for traffic and the platform.

Can I book transportation to a tourist attraction?

Yes. Tell us the attraction and where you are starting from. Some sites have designated drop-off points, so we may agree the nearest practical stop with you.

Can families book city transfers?

Yes. Tell us how many adults and children are travelling, how much luggage you have and whether you need a child seat, so we can suggest a suitable vehicle.

Can I travel with luggage?

Yes. Let us know how many bags you have, and mention anything large such as pushchairs or sports equipment, so the vehicle can be matched to it.

Can I book a city transfer for a business meeting?

Yes. Send the pickup address, the meeting address and the time you need to arrive, and we will work the journey out around it.

What information should I provide when booking?

Pickup address or location, destination, date, time, number of passengers, luggage information, and any special instructions such as a gate, entrance or access notes.
