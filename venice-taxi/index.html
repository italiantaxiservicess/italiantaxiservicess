The one thing to know

Road Access and Water Access Are Two Different Worlds

Before anything else, work out which side of the line your hotel falls on. It decides where a road pickup or drop-off is possible.

Reachable by road

  • Marco Polo Airport (VCE)On the mainland.
  • MestreMainland, with ordinary roads.
  • Piazzale RomaThe main road terminus beside the historic centre.

Reachable on foot or by water

  • The historic centreCanals, bridges and steps; no roads for cars.
  • Venezia Santa LuciaThe railway station, a short walk from Piazzale Roma.
  • Many hotelsOften on a canal or in a lane. Ask us about yours.

We do not imply a road taxi can reach every hotel or destination in Venice, and this page does not describe a water-taxi service. If your hotel needs a boat or a walk, tell us and we will say what we can and cannot arrange.

Hotels & private journeys

Your Hotel Decides the Plan

A hotel in Mestre or near Piazzale Roma can often be reached by road. A hotel deeper in the historic centre usually means a road drop-off at the edge and a walk or a boat from there. Either way, send the exact hotel name and address, and we agree a realistic meeting point with you.

A direct run between two places on the mainland, or on to another city, is a private transfer. Longer journeys are covered under long-distance transfers; we confirm availability for each route and do not list prices or journey times.

Questions

Venice Transfer Questions

Can a road taxi take me to my hotel in Venice?

Only if the hotel can be reached by road. Venice's historic centre has canals instead of streets, so road vehicles stop at its edge, at places such as Piazzale Roma, and the rest of the way is on foot or by water. Tell us your hotel's exact name and address and we will tell you where a road pickup or drop-off is realistic. We do not imply a road taxi can reach every address.

Do you offer water taxis?

This page does not describe a water-taxi service. If you need to reach a hotel only accessible by water, say so in your enquiry and we will tell you what we can and cannot arrange.

Can I enquire about a transfer from Marco Polo Airport (VCE)?

Yes, you can send a request with VCE as your pickup or destination. Whether we can serve it is confirmed when you submit. Add your flight number and time, and where in Venice you are staying. See airport transfers.

Where would I be collected near Venezia Santa Lucia or Piazzale Roma?

Piazzale Roma is the main road terminus, a short walk from Santa Lucia station. Name the place where you want to meet and your arrival time. We confirm with you whether a particular spot can be used; we do not promise one in advance.

I am staying in Mestre. Is that different?

Yes. Mestre is on the mainland and reachable by road, which makes pickups simpler than inside the historic centre. Give the exact hotel address and we plan from there.

How should I plan for luggage in Venice?

Tell us how many bags you have and where you are staying. Bridges and steps make luggage harder in the historic centre, and the last stretch from a road drop-off may be on foot. We do not state luggage capacities; what you send decides what we suggest.

Can I travel from Venice to another Italian city?

You can ask. Send the destination, date and number of travellers, and we will confirm whether we can arrange it. Routes on our pages are examples, not confirmed routes. See long-distance transfers.

How do I ask about a route-specific price?

Send the route through the booking page or ask through the contact page. The website shows no automatic estimated fare and we publish no prices or journey times here. Pricing is confirmed for your specific trip.
