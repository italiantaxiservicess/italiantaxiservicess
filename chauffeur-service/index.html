What it means

More Than Getting From A to B

A taxi takes you to one place. A chauffeur is arranged around a plan.

With a chauffeur service you share your plan in advance: where you start, where you need to be, and the stops in between. A driver is arranged for that journey, so you are not hailing, waiting or re-explaining the route at every stop.

It is most useful on days with more than one leg, when getting from point to point is a task you would rather not manage yourself.

For business travellers

Keep Business Travel Moving

A working trip rarely has one destination. You land, check in, get to a meeting, perhaps attend an event, and head back to the airport. A private driver lets you move through that sequence with one plan instead of a string of separate rides.

  • Planned transportation. The journey is arranged around your schedule.
  • A private environment. Make a call or go over your notes on the way.
  • Less coordination. No booking a new ride for every leg.
  • Easy movement between appointments. One driver for the sequence.
