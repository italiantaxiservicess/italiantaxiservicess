Turin Taxi & Private Transfers

Turin is orderly, arcaded and business-minded, with the Alps on its horizon and two main railway stations that travellers regularly confuse. A pre-arranged private ride works best when the details are precise: which station, which hotel, which venue, which time. The classic Turin mix-up

Porta Nuova or Porta Susa?

Turin has two principal railway stations, and arriving at one while your driver waits at the other is the most avoidable mistake. Check your ticket, name the right station, and add your train time.

Porta Nuova

The historic central station. Many visitors arrive here, close to the heart of the city.

Name the exit you will use

Porta Susa

The other major station, on the line through the city. High-speed and regional trains may call here.

Check your ticket first

We confirm with you whether a particular pickup spot at either station can be used, and we do not promise one in advance. We state no waiting times.

Business & exhibitions

Turin for Work: Fixed Times, Fixed Venues

Hotels & streets

Where Exactly Is the Hotel?

Turin's centre is a regular grid of long streets and arcades, which makes directions easy but pickup points less obvious. Some streets and squares have restricted access, and we make no claims about the rules.

Send your hotel's exact name and street, and tell us if there are several entrances. We then agree a realistic pickup or drop-off point rather than promising the doorstep. With children or several suitcases, say so: the last stretch may be on foot. See group transfers for larger parties.

Questions

Turin Transfer Questions

How do I request a transfer from Turin Airport (TRN)?

Send a request on the booking page with TRN as your pickup, plus your flight number and arrival time, your destination in or around Turin, passengers and luggage. Whether we can serve TRN is confirmed when you submit, and the meeting point is agreed with you; we do not describe waiting time here. See airport transfers.

Which station should I name, Porta Nuova or Porta Susa?

Name the one your train actually uses; they are different stations, and mixing them up is the commonest source of confusion. Add your train time and the exit where you will wait. We confirm with you whether a particular spot can be used.

Can you collect me from a hotel in the city centre?

You can enquire. Send the hotel's exact name and street. Parts of the centre have restricted access, and we make no claims about the rules, so we agree a realistic pickup point with you instead of promising the doorstep.

I am travelling to Turin for a trade fair or business meeting. What should I tell you?

The venue's exact name and address, the time you must arrive, and your pickup point. Give the arrival time first and the plan works backwards from it. We do not guarantee punctuality. See city transfers.

Can I enquire about travel from Turin to Milan, the Alps or another city?

Yes, you can ask. Name the destination, the date and the group size, and we confirm whether we can arrange it. Places mentioned on this page are examples of enquiries, not confirmed routes, and we publish no journey times or prices. See long-distance transfers.

What if my flight or train arrives late?

Include your flight or train number so the journey is planned around it. We do not describe flight monitoring or waiting policies on this site, so please ask what can be agreed for your trip when you enquire.

Can a family or a group book a transfer in Turin?

Yes. Tell us how many adults and children are travelling, any child seat need, and your luggage. We state no vehicle capacities; what you send decides what we suggest. See group transfers.

What should I include when asking for a quote?

Exact pickup and destination, travel date, pickup time, number of passengers and luggage, plus flight or train details. The website shows no automatic estimated fare; pricing is confirmed for your specific journey.

What is the difference between a city transfer and a private transfer?

A city transfer is a shorter ride within the city, such as hotel to station. A private transfer is a direct ride between two places you choose, which can also run beyond the city.

Where can I see your other destinations?

The service areas page lists them, with dedicated pages for Milan and Rome, among others.
