Florence • Tuscany

Florence Taxi & Private Transfers

Florence is best seen slowly, but getting in and out of it rarely is: a flight at the edge of the city, a hotel inside streets built long before cars, a train to catch, luggage on cobbles. A pre-arranged private ride can take some of that guesswork away. Send us your journey and we confirm what we can arrange; Florence is not yet listed as a confirmed service area.

Why arrange a ride

A Compact City With Awkward Access

Everything in Florence feels close, and in a sense it is. What is awkward is the last few hundred metres: narrow lanes, busy squares and areas where vehicle access is limited. That is exactly where a clear plan helps.

Pre-arranged transport suits visitors arriving after a long journey, families with children and bags, business travellers with a set time to keep, and anyone who would rather not work out the route on arrival. It is not always the cheapest or fastest option, and we do not claim it is.

Florence on the map

Where Travellers Ask to Go

These are common starting points and destinations. We do not promise direct drop-off at any of them; where access is limited, we agree a sensible meeting point with you.

  1. Santa Maria Novella station

    The main railway station, where many visitors arrive. Name the exit you will use and your train time.

  2. Florence Cathedral (the Duomo)

    The red dome and its piazza anchor most visits. Crowds and pedestrian areas mean a nearby meeting point is more realistic than the doorstep.

  3. Ponte Vecchio and the Arno

    The old bridge sits at the heart of the centre and the Oltrarno across the river. A named street or hotel is the best pickup description here.

  4. The historic centre

    Hotels, galleries and restaurants share very narrow streets. Give the hotel's exact name and address.

  5. Hotel areas outside the core

    Larger hotels sit on the edges and the hills, where access is easier. Tell us which one and we plan from there.

Airport enquiries

Florence Airport, FLR

If you are flying into or out of Florence Airport, you can send a request with FLR as your pickup or destination. This is an enquiry, not a confirmation: which airports we can serve is confirmed when you submit. We do not describe pickup procedures, waiting allowances, flight monitoring or prices here.

Many travellers also fly to other airports and continue to Florence. For that, ask about a longer journey, or read about airport transfers.

Beyond the city

From Florence Into Tuscany

For many visitors, Florence is a base. The hills, villages and smaller cities of Tuscany are a short way out, and a private ride can link them without changing transport. You can enquire about trips to nearby towns by naming the place, the date and the group size.

We do not state which Tuscan destinations we serve, nor journey times or prices. The places below are examples of the kind of enquiry, not confirmed routes.

Hotels & city journeys

Between Your Hotel and the Rest of Your Day

Most Florence rides are short: hotel to station, a restaurant across the river, a morning at the galleries. Those fall under city transfers. A direct run between two places, such as a hotel in the hills to your next stop, is closer to a private transfer.

Visiting with children or several suitcases? Say so when you write. Narrow streets and stairs make luggage matter more here than in many cities.

Questions

Florence Transfer Questions

How do I request a taxi or private transfer in Florence?

Use the booking page: pick a service type, enter your pickup, destination, date, time and passengers, and send it. We review it and contact you with the next steps. Florence is not yet listed as a confirmed service area, so we confirm what we can arrange once we see your journey.

Can I enquire about a transfer from Florence Airport (FLR)?

Yes, you can send a request with FLR as your pickup or destination. Whether we can serve it is confirmed when you submit. Add your flight number and arrival or departure time, and your hotel's full name and address. See airport transfers.

What if my hotel is inside the historic centre?

Tell us the hotel's exact name and street. Florence's old centre has narrow streets and areas with access limits, so the practical meeting point may be a short walk away. We agree a sensible pickup or drop-off point with you rather than promising the doorstep.

Can I arrange a pickup near Santa Maria Novella station?

You can enquire. Name the station, the side or entrance where you will wait, and your train time. We confirm with you whether a particular spot can be used; we do not promise one in advance.

Can I ask about trips to nearby Tuscan places from Florence?

Yes. Send the place, the date and how many are travelling, and we will confirm whether we can arrange it. Places mentioned on this page are examples, not confirmed routes. See long-distance transfers.

I am travelling with a lot of luggage. What should I tell you?

Give the number of bags and mention anything large, plus the number of passengers. Narrow streets and stairs make luggage matter more in the centre. We do not state capacities; the details you send decide what we suggest. See group transfers for larger parties.

How do I ask about a route-specific price?

Send the route through the booking page or ask through the contact page. The website does not show an automatic estimated fare, and we publish no prices or journey times here. Pricing is confirmed for your specific trip.
